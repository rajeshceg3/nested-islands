Sources & notes

Chains and coordinates follow Wikipedia's "Recursive islands and lakes" catalogue, cross-checked against the sources below. "Largest" and "deepest" mean within that community-maintained catalogue, not a surveyed record.

Satellite imagery: EOxCloudless Sentinel-2 mosaics by EOX (2016 and 2021 vintages, labeled on screen) and, for the US stop, USGS The National Map (public domain). Mosaics are blends of a year's passes, not a photo of today.
